THE CROSSING (MAP): TEXTURES
============================

table.jpg  The wooden table the folded chart lies on (the hero's backdrop).

Both were generated by the owner (in their own Claude chat) for this
design; they carry no third-party licence. No photographs of people or
places ship: the design's stock passage, story, folio and reply photos
were left out, and each moment of the day is drawn as a piece of the chart
(luma-map.js) unless the couple adds their own photo.
