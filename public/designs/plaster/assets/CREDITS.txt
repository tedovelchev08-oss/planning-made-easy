PLASTER — credits for the grounds

Both files here come from Pexels, under the Pexels licence: free for
commercial use, no attribution required, modification permitted. Selling
unaltered copies is not permitted, which is why each has been cropped and
colour-graded at file level. Neither shows a person or a place.

wall.jpg
  Source   Pexels photo 11236688, close-up of an orange stucco wall.
  Change   Graded toward warm terracotta (saturation +68%, warm channel
           balance), resized to 1400x1400.

stock.jpg  (the envelope and card paper)
  Source   Pexels photo 20899958, textured white paper.
