AMALFI — the painted scenes
===========================

coast.svg  the coast seen through the arch at the top of the page
view.svg   the sea behind the shutters

Both were drawn for this design (generated from code, no photograph or
third-party artwork in them), so they ship with it. The original standalone
design hot-linked two Unsplash photographs here; Luma does not, so a guest's
phone asks nothing of another host. Either file can be swapped for a
generated image of the same shape (coast about 16:9, view 3:4) by changing
--img-coast / --img-view in wedding-design.css.
