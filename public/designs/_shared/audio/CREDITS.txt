BACKGROUND MUSIC — SOURCES AND LICENCES
=======================================

Every track here is Public Domain or CC0. That covers BOTH layers of rights
that a recording carries:

  1. the composition  (all five composers died well over a century ago)
  2. the performance  (a recording has its own copyright, separate from the
                       piece — this is the part that is easy to get wrong)

All five were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, where each file's licence is
stated on its own file page, and re-encoded to MP3. The originals were .ogg
and .opus, which Safari does not reliably play, and most wedding guests open
these invitations on an iPhone.

ENCODING
--------

MP3, 96 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo, with a 4-second fade at the end so the loop
does not cut off mid-note. All five were normalised to -19 LUFS, which is a
background level and identical across tracks, so switching selection in the
builder never jumps in volume.


IF YOU ADD A TRACK
------------------

Check the RECORDING's licence, not just the composer's dates. "Bach is out of
copyright" says nothing about the 2019 recording of it. The set this replaced
was hotlinked to Internet Archive uploads named "ClairDeLuneFromTwilight" and
"CannonInDFromDudeWhereIsMyCar" — soundtrack rips, still fully in copyright,
shipping inside a product sold to hundreds of couples.
