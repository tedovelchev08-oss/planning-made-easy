MIRROR PALACE — BACKGROUND MUSIC
================================

THIS FOLDER IS NOT SHARED.

The other designs keep their music in assets/audio/, which
sync-core-to-designs.bat mirrors into every design folder. This one is
assets/music/ deliberately, so these three tracks belong to Mirror Palace
alone and a core sync cannot overwrite them or add the shared five back.

design.js overrides the core's resolveTrack() to point at this folder. The
shared wedding-core.js is untouched.


WHY THESE THREE
---------------

The shared set is five solo piano pieces — Chopin, Debussy, Satie, Schumann,
Ravel. All French and German Romantic, all intimate and dreamy. That suits a
garden or a night sky. This design is Italian, black tie, architectural:
gilt, candlelight, mirrors, Bodoni.

Bach answers that. The Goldberg Variations are one theme reflected thirty
ways — symmetry, inversion, return — which is this design's whole idea in
musical form. Formal rather than sentimental, and nocturnal: the Goldbergs
were written, by legend, for a count who could not sleep.

All three are the same pianist, so the set holds together, and the three
differ enough in temperature that the picker is not monotonous.

BOTH LAYERS OF RIGHTS ARE COVERED
---------------------------------

  1. the composition — Bach died in 1750
  2. the performance — a recording carries its own copyright, separate from
     the piece. This is the layer that is easy to get wrong. Both Open Bach
     projects were crowdfunded specifically to place studio recordings in
     the public domain under CC0.

These are real studio performances on a Bösendorfer, not MIDI renderings.


REJECTED — DO NOT RE-LITIGATE
-----------------------------

Two obvious-looking candidates were checked and dropped:

  Marcello — Adagio from the Oboe Concerto in D minor
    The Commons recording is public domain, but it is a MIDI mockup built
    from the "Evanessence2" oboe library and "Titanic 200 GS" strings. It
    sounds synthetic, which is wrong for a product sold as luxury.

  Saint-Saëns — Le Cygne (John Michel, cello)
    CC BY-SA 3.0, not public domain. ShareAlike requires derivative works
    carry the same licence, which is a bad fit for a paid template, and it
    breaks the clean PD/CC0 rule the other tracks follow.

  Albinoni — Adagio in G minor
    Not Albinoni. Remo Giazotto composed it in 1958 and died in 1998, so the
    composition itself is still in copyright regardless of who performs it.
    It sounds exactly right for this design, which is why it is worth
    writing down that it cannot be used.


PROCESSING
----------

Re-encoded from the CC0 sources with ffmpeg:

  - MP3, because Safari does not reliably play .ogg or .opus and guests are
    overwhelmingly on iPhones
  - normalised to -19 LUFS, so switching tracks in the builder never jumps
    in volume — matches the shared set's level
  - mono, 96 kbps: this is ambient background under a web page, not a
    listening copy

Regenerate with _fetch-music.sh in the design folder.
Total: 8.8 MB.
