# Design audit, 2026-11-15 (parked)

Phone-size (375x812) pass over the 16 invitation designs and the Golden Hour prototype, with a
full sample couple and no photo. Stills only: openings and motion were not judged. Parked by the
owner the same day in favour of a code audit; pick up from "Do first".

## Verdict
Most designs are a strong first screen on the same page (details, story, schedule, questions,
form, footer). Only Celestial, Red Thread, Riviera, The Tide and The Inscription carry their idea
through to the reply. Glasshouse and Plaster should be retired or rebuilt. Luxe holds three of the
weaker designs (All In, Liquid Gold, Picture House).

## Visible bugs
- Liquid Gold: the floating "A & B / Menu" has no backing and collides with text while scrolling.
- All In: the fanned cards cover the top of the first name at phone width; empty grey square on
  the Ace when there is no photo.
- Art Deco: a solid black block where the story photo goes when there is no photo.
- Palm Springs: footer prints "Palm Springs" on any wedding (reads as the place).
- Golden Hour: the clock pill sits on top of each heading as it scrolls under.
- The Crossing: chart labels looked mirrored in a still; check on a phone.

## Patterns
- 15 of 16 designs have no link-preview image, and none shows the couple's names in the preview.
- 12 of 17 have 17+ pieces of text under 12px (tracked capitals).
- About 30 font families across the set; Celestial still uses Italiana.
- Eight designs print the design's product name in the guest footer.
- Empty-photo states unfinished: Art Deco, All In, Red Thread, Amalfi.
- English only; three different date formats.

## Do first
1. Per-couple link preview (names, date, the design's look).
2. Fix the visible bugs above.
3. 12px floor for text in every design.
4. Remove design names from guest footers.
5. Phone-check the openings of Palm Springs, Palm Shadow, Tide, Picture House, Liquid Gold,
   Inscription, The Crossing; decide on Glasshouse.

## Ideas kept for later
Guest's name set into the material; the reply as each design's finale; a living invitation that
fills as guests say yes; day-of mode from the schedule; "sunsets to go" on return visits; the
couple's photo printed in the design's material; tilt to move the light; ambient sound, off by
default; the couple's own closing line instead of the design name.
