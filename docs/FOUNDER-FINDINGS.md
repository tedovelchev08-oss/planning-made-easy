# Twofold: business findings (founder pack, November 2026)

A summary of one run of the [founder skill pack](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/founder-skill) on Twofold: a board review, a competitor map, a 100-buyer simulated panel, pricing, the offer, the numbers, marketing, brand, operations and a launch plan. The full working files stay local in `founder/` (gitignored); this page is the readable version.

> **Read this with care.** The buyer panel is **AI-simulated buyers, not real couples**; simulated buyers lean agreeable, so treat their buy rate as an upper bound. Every money figure is a **projection** from estimates marked as such. Real couples and real quotes confirm or overturn all of it. Not financial, legal or tax advice.

## The verdict: Not yet

Computed by the pack's tool, not written by hand:

- ✓ Year 1 operating profit: **€2,327** (projection).
- ✗ **8 of 100** simulated buyers buy (8%; the method's bar is 25%).

Then run a real test with real prices (see *Launch*), which replaces the simulated buy rate.

**The one thing to do next:** search the **TWOFOLD** trademark ([EUIPO](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/), [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/)); if clear, register **twofold.wedding**. Everything else waits on the domain.

## 1. The board: 2 "fund if", 1 pass (average 4.3 / 10)

Three reviewers, each applying one published framework (offers, monopoly, product). They are lenses, not the people they come from.

| Lens | Score | Vote |
|---|---:|---|
| Offers | 4 | Fund if |
| Monopoly | 3 | Pass |
| Product | 6 | Fund if |

**Risks more than one raised:**
1. **No way to reach couples:** €0 marketing, no domain, no email, no named channel. Couples buy once and leave after the wedding.
2. **Free incumbents** (Zola, Bridebook, Joy, Bodas.net) give the planner, website and RSVPs away; the paid plans must win on the invitation alone.
3. **The invitation looks borrowed:** a €49–€99 invitation from a vercel.app address undercuts the luxury promise.
4. **No proof** and an unknown upgrade rate.

**Their strongest version of the business:** *an invitation business with a free planner around it*, not a planner with invitations on top. The planner earns trust; the invitation earns the money; every invitation sent puts the brand in front of a guest list.

**Conditions:** own domain and email before charging; a guarantee; a cheap real test of the upgrade rate; measure whether invitations bring new couples; watch 10 real couples through their first five minutes; test the animated designs on mid-range phones with sound and motion off.

## 2. Competitors

| Name | Type | Price for a paid invitation with RSVP | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Greenvelope](https://www.greenvelope.com/resources/how-much-does-greenvelope-cost) | direct | $99 up to 100 guests, $159 up to 160, $249 up to 250 | premium digital invitations, RSVP, reminders (4.9 on ~10,000 Trustpilot reviews) |
| [Specially Love](https://specially.love/en/wedding-invitations) | direct (EU) | €69 self-made, €109 designed for you | digital invitation with RSVP, music |
| [Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing) | direct | per guest, in "coins"; Pro from $250 a year | digital invitations |
| [Joy](https://withjoy.com/) | direct | free | website, RSVP, invitations, registry, app |
| [Weddybird](https://weddybird.com/en/pricing) | direct (EU) | free basic; €69 for 12 months | wedding website with RSVP |
| [Bridebook](https://bridebook.com/uk) | indirect | free | planner, guest list, RSVP, supplier search |
| [Bodas.net](https://www.bodas.net/) | indirect (EU) | free | planner, seating, website, vendor directory |
| Canva + WhatsApp | substitute | — | DIY invitation, replies by message |

**The gap:** nobody found sells a **designed, paid invitation inside a plan both partners share, at one flat price**. At €49 for every guest, Celebration is below every paid invitation found.

**Complaints worth answering:** paying per guest feels greedy (Paperless Post); phone app weaker than the website (Joy); guest lists tedious to edit (Bridebook); vendor spam.

**Fastest copier:** Joy (has everything but premium designs); Greenvelope (could add a light planner); Bridebook / Bodas.net (could sell designs to millions of free users).

## 3. The buyer panel: 8 buy · 92 pass

100 simulated EU couples, aged 25–38, weighted toward affluent and design-led couples at the founder's request.

**Who buys:**

| Group | Buy rate |
|---|---:|
| Last-minute (invitations not sent, under 4 months) | 62% |
| Design-led | 12% |
| Mid-size wedding (80–150 guests) | 11% |
| Spreadsheet planners, already on a free planner, family/planner runs it, budget DIY, review readers, traditionalists | 0% |

Every buyer had **more than 50 guests** (the free plan ran out).

**Why they pass:**

| Reason | Share |
|---|---:|
| Habit (already on Bodas.net / Joy / Bridebook or a Google Sheet) | 42% |
| Trust (unknown brand, no reviews, vercel.app address, guest data) | 33% |
| Need (printing anyway; someone else runs the guest list) | 11% |
| Quality (can't judge designs from a sentence) | 7% |
| Price | 7% |

**What would flip a no** (most repeated):
- the **same design as a printable card with a QR code**, paper and link replies in one list;
- **own domain** and a clear privacy page (who runs it, where data is, deleted after the wedding);
- **reviews and real examples**;
- **preview the designs with our own colours and photo** before paying;
- **invitations in two languages**;
- **import from a Google Sheet / Bodas.net / Joy**;
- **let a parent or the planner edit** the guest list.

*Known flaws in this run:* the "last-minute" trait could be dealt alongside "wedding is nine months away"; incomes printed with a `$` though they were euros.

## 4. Pricing

**Acceptable range from the panel: €14–€69.** Least resistance €15; bargain/expensive crossover €35; median "too expensive" €90.

| Plan | Price | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Engaged | free, up to 50 guests | keep; say the 50-guest limit **upfront** (some buyers called it "a trap") |
| Celebration | **€49 once** | keep: inside the range, below every paid competitor; price is rarely why people pass |
| Luxe | **€99 once** | keep, but sell to affluent couples only (their median "too expensive" was €200; mid-size couples' was €90) |

**Founding offer:** Celebration **€39**, Luxe **€79**, for the **first 50 paying couples or until 31 March 2027**, then €49 / €99. Said plainly; no fake "was" price.

**Test in sequence** (no ad traffic to split): €39 for the first 50, €49 for the next 50; compare how many couples past 50 guests pay.

## 5. The offer

**Already exists but wasn't in the pitch:** 14-day full refund; guest list import and export (CSV); a privacy policy.

**The stack (what to build, in this order):**
1. **Try every design free with your own names, colours and photo; pay only to send.**
2. **Own domain and email** for every invitation link.
3. **A plain privacy summary** (don't claim "stored only in the EU": some providers are US-based under EU safeguards).
4. **The QR reply code as files any stationer can print** (formal couples reject a home-print PDF).
5. **Invite a helper:** a parent or planner edits the guest list.
6. **Guest-day promise:** full refund any time before the wedding if guests can't open the invitation or reply because of a fault on our side.

**Later:** two-language invitations; Google Sheets sync; a done-for-you Luxe at a higher price.

**Re-test on the new offer (20 buyers): 4 buy · 16 pass (20%)**, against 8% before.

| Reason to pass | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Habit | 42% | 44% |
| Trust | 33% | 19% |
| Need | 11% | 25% |

Trust roughly halved. Design-led couples moved most (3 of 5 bought; thin sample). Habit didn't move at all.

- **Honest scale:** this is a side income at the planned volume, not a salary.

## 7. Marketing (€0 budget)

**First audience:** design-led couples, 80–150 guests, 6–9 months out, who save invitations on Pinterest/Instagram. Then last-minute couples.

**Positioning:** *For couples who care how their wedding looks, Twofold is the invitation studio that lets you try every design with your own names and photo for free, and pay once only when you love it.* Keep "Two of you. One plan." as the brand line.

**Channels:** Pinterest; Instagram Reels / TikTok (an invitation opening on a phone); the "Made with Twofold" footer on every invitation. Planners in month 2. **Not doing:** paid ads, SEO (until the domain is live).

**Beachhead:** the planner and invitations have **no Bulgarian translation yet**. Start with English-speaking EU couples online; translate to Bulgarian in parallel.

**Ten hooks** (each answers an objection), e.g.: *"Your names. Your photo. Before you pay a cent."* · *"Grandma gets the card. Everyone else gets the link. One list."* · *"Free up to 50 guests. €49 once after that. No subscription."*

**Most a paid couple is worth:** €37.68 → at most ~€3 per sign-up (at the panel's 8%).

**Watch weekly:** sign-ups (under 10/week by week 4 → change channels); share of couples past 50 guests who pay (under 1 in 10 → fix the offer); sign-ups from invitation links (zero after 10 live invitations → the loop doesn't exist yet).

## 8. Brand

**Keep Twofold.** It fits the couple and the quiet-luxury look; the tagline and first screen must carry "invitation".

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Trademark | **to do**: EUIPO / TMview, classes 9, 42, 35. Other apps use the name (an AI medical scribe, a puzzle game, a camera app); none in weddings |
| twofold.com / .co / .love | in use |
| twofold.app | parked with a domain marketplace |
| **twofold.wedding**, twofold.eu, twofold.bg, twofoldwedding.com | no DNS found: possibly free, **confirm at a registrar** |

**Recommendation:** register **twofold.wedding** (`twofold.wedding/ana-and-boris`) + **twofold.eu**.

**Promise:** *Your invitation, exactly as you'll send it, before you pay, and every reply in one list you both see.*
**Voice:** quiet, exact, warm.
**Contrast check:** champagne text on light paper fails (1.8:1); keep champagne for dark grounds and rings.

## 9. Operations (Bulgaria, one person)

- **Week:** ~15 hours (marketing 8, support 3, fixes 3, admin 1).
- **Payments:** Stripe (1.5% + €0.25) vs a merchant of record (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy, 5% + 50¢), which files EU VAT for you. That's worth weighing while there's no company or accountant.
- **Checklist:**
  - [ ] Choose a legal form (sole trader ЕТ or ЕООД) and register at the Registry Agency.
  - [ ] Tax registration at НАП; ask about VAT timing.
  - [ ] EU One-Stop Shop above €10,000 a year of cross-border consumer sales (or use a merchant of record).
  - [ ] GDPR record of processing; check with КЗЛД or a lawyer.
  - [ ] Seller's name in the terms.
  - [ ] Stripe in live mode.
- **Top risks:**
  1. Guests can't open an invitation (high impact): test on two phones after every change.
  2. VAT mistakes: get an accountant or a merchant of record.
  3. Slow months: costs are tiny; review channels at week 4.
  4. A trademark conflict: search before buying the domain.

## 10. Launch: Monday 11 January 2027

**The test (from 30 November):** a founding-price waitlist on the own domain. The free plan stays open; the upgrade button says "tell me when I can pay".

**Success line:**
- By 10 January: **at least 60 sign-ups**, **15 couples past 50 guests**, and **10 asking to pay**.
- By 25 January: **5 paid founding couples**.
- If under 1 in 10 couples past 50 guests pay, change the offer, not the marketing.

| Week | Key tasks (★ critical path) |
|---|---|
| 16–22 Nov | ★ trademark search, ★ register domain; first accountant call; apply migration 0021 (registry on the invitation) if not done yet |
| 23–29 Nov | ★ domain on Vercel, Vercel Pro; Resend email; privacy summary |
| 30 Nov–6 Dec | ★ start the waitlist test; build free design previews |
| 7–13 Dec | ★ register the company; bank account; `src=` tracking on links |
| 14–20 Dec | ★ Stripe live (or merchant of record); terms updated; QR reply files |
| 21–27 Dec | live €39 test purchase + refund; phone tests |
| 28 Dec–3 Jan | marketing calendar begins |
| 4–10 Jan | friends-and-family soft open (Thu 7 Jan) |
| **11 Jan** | **launch** |

**If the company or Stripe live isn't done by 3 January, move the launch, not the promises.**

**Reviews:**
- **Day 7:** did couples manage alone?
- **Day 14:** did the test hit 5 paid couples?
- **Day 30:** paid couples vs break-even (2 a month), and real conversion vs the panel's 8%.
