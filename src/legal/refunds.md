# Refund policy

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This policy explains when and how you can get your money back for a Twofold plan. It is part of our [terms of service](#/terms).

## The short version

- **14 days, no questions asked.** You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying a plan, for any reason.
- **Nothing is deleted.** If you get a refund, your wedding and everything in it stay in Twofold. Only the paid features are locked again.
- **Ask by email** at **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com**.

## 1. Your 14-day right to a refund

You can cancel any plan purchase within **14 days** of the day you bought it, without giving a reason, and get a **full refund**. This applies whether or not you have already used the plan's features.

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, this is your legal **right of withdrawal**. We give the same 14 days to everyone, wherever they live.

## 2. How to ask for a refund

Email **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com** from the email address of your Twofold account, within 14 days of your purchase. Tell us:

- that you want to cancel your purchase (for example: "I withdraw from my purchase of the Celebration plan"); and
- the wedding it is for, if you plan more than one.

You don't have to give a reason, but feedback helps us improve Twofold.

If you prefer, you can use this model form. You don't have to use it:

*To Teodor Yovchev Velchev (Twofold), lumaplanning.support@gmail.com: I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the following service: (plan name), bought on (date). Name: (your name). Email address of the account: (email). Date: (date).*

## 3. How you are refunded

- We refund the **full amount you paid** for that purchase, to the **card or payment method you used**, through Stripe.
- We send the refund **within 14 days** of receiving your request. Depending on your bank, it can take another 5 to 10 working days to show in your account.
- There is no fee for a refund.

## 4. What happens to your wedding after a refund

- Your wedding goes back to the **highest plan you have still paid for**, or to the free **Engaged** plan.
  For example, if you bought Celebration and then upgraded to Luxe, and you cancel the Luxe upgrade, you keep Celebration.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Your guests, designs, answers and plans all stay.
- Features of the refunded plan are locked again. For example, on the free plan you can't add guests beyond 50, but guests you already added stay.
- You can buy the plan again at any time.

## 5. After 14 days

After the 14-day period, purchases are generally **not refundable**, because the plan has been delivered and you can keep using it.

This doesn't affect your rights under consumer law. If Twofold doesn't work as described and we can't fix it within a reasonable time, you may be entitled to a price reduction or a refund. Contact us and we will look at it fairly. We may also give a refund or a partial refund as a goodwill gesture; if we give a partial refund, you keep your plan.

## 6. Problems with a payment

If you see a charge you don't recognise, or something went wrong with a payment, please email us **before** disputing it with your bank. We can usually sort it out faster. If a payment is reversed through your bank (a "chargeback"), we may move the wedding back to the plan it had before that payment.
