# Privacy policy

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how **Teodor Yovchev Velchev**, doing business as **Twofold** ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**"), collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use Twofold, the online wedding planner at [planning-made-easy.vercel.app](https://planning-made-easy.vercel.app) (the "**Services**"), including when you:

- create an account and plan a wedding;
- answer a wedding invitation as a guest;
- buy a plan; or
- contact us.

We decide how your personal information is processed, so we are its controller. If you have questions, email **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com**.

## Summary

- **What we collect:** the information you give us (your email address and the wedding details you enter), information couples enter about their guests and vendors, guests' own invitation answers, and basic technical information (such as your IP address) needed to run and protect the Services.
- **Sensitive information:** guests' dietary requirements may reveal health or religious information. See section 4.
- **What we don't do:** we don't sell personal information, show advertising, send marketing emails, or use cookies.
- **Who receives it:** only the service providers that run Twofold for us (see section 6).
- **Your rights:** you can export your data at any time from the app, and ask us to correct or delete it.

## 1. What information do we collect?

**Information you give us.** When you create an account and use the Services, we collect:

- your **email address** and **password** (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it);
- your and your partner's **names**, the wedding **date**, **venue** and **location**, and your preferences (language, currency, time zone);
- everything you enter while planning: guests, seating, budget, vendors, tasks, gift list, invitation and website designs, photos and songs you upload;
- if you invite a partner to plan with you, their **email address**.

**Payment information.** When you buy a plan, you enter your card and billing details on **Stripe's** payment page. Twofold never receives or stores your card details. We receive a record of the purchase from Stripe (the plan, amount, currency, date and Stripe's payment reference). Stripe's privacy notice: [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

**Information about other people.** Couples enter information about their guests and vendors. See section 4.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Services, our hosting provider receives technical information such as your **IP address**, browser and device type, and the pages and features requested. We use the **country** your IP address belongs to only to show prices in a suitable currency; we don't store it, and we don't collect your precise location. When something goes wrong, the app sends us an **error report** (the error message, the page it happened on, and your browser type).

All information you give us must be true and accurate, and you should keep it up to date.

## 2. How do we use your information?

We use personal information only to:

- **create and run your account**, and let you sign in;
- **provide the Services**: store your wedding plans, show your invitation to your guests, collect and show their answers, and let you plan together with a partner;
- **process purchases** and refunds, and give you the plan you paid for;
- **keep the Services secure**, prevent spam and abuse (for example, fake invitation answers), and find and fix errors;
- **reply to you** when you contact us; and
- **comply with the law**, for example accounting and tax rules.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, marketing emails, profiling or automated decisions.

## 3. What legal bases do we rely on? (EU and UK)

- **Contract:** to create and run your account, store your wedding plans, process purchases and provide the features you use.
- **Legitimate interests:** to keep the Services secure, stop spam invitation answers, and find and fix errors. We use as little information as these purposes need: for example, we store only a one-way code made from a guest's IP address, never the address itself.
- **Legal obligation:** to keep payment and accounting records.
- **Consent:** where we ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; this doesn't affect processing that happened before.

## 4. Information about guests and other people

Couples use Twofold to plan their wedding, which includes entering information about **other people**:

- **Guests:** names, party or household, whether they are attending, meal choices, dietary requirements, notes, plus-ones and seating.
- **Vendors:** company names and contact details (name, email address, phone number).
- **A partner:** the email address of the partner a couple invites to plan with them.

Guests can also answer an invitation themselves through a link the couple shares. We store the guest's answer for that couple: their name, attendance, meal choice, an optional note and an optional plus-one. Guests don't need a Twofold account, and **we don't contact guests**: couples send invitations themselves, by email or their own messaging apps.

**Dietary requirements and notes** may reveal information about a person's health (such as allergies) or beliefs (such as halal or kosher meals). These fields are optional. They are used only to show the couple what to serve, they are visible only to the couple planning the wedding, and they are deleted with the wedding.

Couples should only add the information they need to plan their wedding. If you are a guest or a vendor and want your information corrected or removed, contact the couple, or email us at lumaplanning.support@gmail.com and we will help.

## 5. Invitation links and uploaded files

When a couple opens their invitation to guests, **anyone with the invitation link** can see the details the couple chose to show: their names, the date, the venue, the design and any photo or song they uploaded. Until the couple opens it, the link shows only that the invitation isn't open yet.

**Personal invitation links** contain a private code. Anyone with the link can answer for that guest, so couples should share each link only with its guest.

Uploaded photos and songs are stored at an address that works for anyone who has it. Photos are resized in your browser before upload, which also removes the information phones store inside a photo, such as where it was taken. When a couple deletes or replaces a photo or song, it is removed from storage straight away, but a cached copy can stay reachable at the same address for up to one hour.

## 6. Who do we share information with?

We don't sell personal information or share it with advertisers. We share it only with the service providers that run Twofold for us. They process it on our instructions, under data processing agreements, and only to provide their service:

- **Supabase** (Supabase Inc.) stores the Twofold database, accounts and uploaded files, and sends sign-in and account emails.
- **Vercel** (Vercel Inc.) hosts the website and its server functions. It receives every request made to Twofold, including your IP address and browser details, and keeps short-term technical logs.
- **Stripe** (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, and its affiliates) processes payments and refunds.
- **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare, Inc.) provides Turnstile, a spam check shown to guests when they answer an invitation. It receives the guest's IP address and browser signals for that check.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business (in which case this policy continues to apply to your information).

## 7. International transfers

Some of our service providers (Supabase, Vercel, Stripe and Cloudflare) are based in the United States or may process data there. When personal information leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

## 8. Cookies and storage on your device

**Twofold doesn't set cookies.** To work, it stores a few items in your browser's local storage:

- your **sign-in session**;
- an **offline copy** of your planner and any **edits not yet saved** (both removed when you sign out);
- the **currency** you chose; and
- for the length of your visit, the **plan you picked** before signing in.

These are strictly necessary for features you use, so we don't ask for consent for them. Twofold uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Cloudflare Turnstile (on the guest answer page) and Stripe (on its own payment page) may store their own data on your device for security and fraud prevention, under their own policies. See also our [cookie policy](#/cookies).

## 9. How long do we keep information?

- **Your account and wedding** (including guests, answers and uploads): until you delete your account or the wedding is deleted.
- **Information stored in your browser:** removed when you sign out. Your chosen currency stays until you clear your browser data.
- **Spam-protection records for guest answers** (a one-way code made from the IP address, never the address itself): deleted after one day.
- **Server and error logs:** a short period, set by our hosting provider.
- **Payment records:** kept by Stripe, and for as long as Bulgarian accounting and tax law requires. If you delete your account, Twofold's own record of a payment is kept without the link to you or your wedding.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous. Copies in backups are deleted as the backups expire.

## 10. How do we keep information safe?

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. They include encrypted connections, access rules that let each couple see only their own wedding, and a spam check on invitation answers. However, no method of storing or sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that unauthorised people will never be able to access it.

## 11. Children

The Services are not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email lumaplanning.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Canada or some US states), you may have the right to:

- **access** your personal information and get a copy of it;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- **receive it in a portable format**; and
- **withdraw consent**, where we rely on consent.

**How to use them:**

- **Export:** use **Export my data** in the account menu to download everything in your wedding at any time.
- **Correct:** edit your information directly in the planner.
- **Delete your account:** use **Delete my account** in the account menu. It takes effect straight away. If you can't, email **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with and we will complete the deletion within 30 days. If you are a wedding's **owner**, deleting your account deletes the whole wedding, including its uploaded photos and songs, and for your partner. If you are a **partner**, only your access is removed. A record of any payment (plan, amount, date and payment reference, no longer linked to you) is kept for as long as accounting law requires.
- **Anything else:** email **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com**. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will reply within one month.

We give these rights to every user, wherever they live, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

**Complaints.** If you think we have handled your information unlawfully, you can complain to a data protection authority. Our lead authority is the **Commission for Personal Data Protection** (Комисия за защита на личните данни), Sofia, Bulgaria, [www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg). You can also complain to the authority in your own EU country ([list of EU authorities](https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)), to the UK **Information Commissioner's Office** ([ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint)), or in Switzerland to the **Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner** ([edoeb.admin.ch](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch)). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

## 13. United States residents

Residents of some US states (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others) have the rights listed in section 12: to know, access, correct and delete their personal information, to get a copy of it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

In the past 12 months, we have collected these categories of personal information:

- **Identifiers** (such as name, email address, IP address): yes.
- **Commercial information** (purchase history): yes.
- **Internet or network activity** (technical logs): yes, kept briefly.
- **Sensitive personal information** (dietary requirements entered for guests): yes, if a couple enters it.

To use your rights, email **lumaplanning.support@gmail.com**. You can ask someone you authorise to make a request for you; we may ask for proof of their authority. If we refuse a request, you can **appeal** by emailing the same address. We will reply in writing with our reasons, and if the appeal is refused, you can complain to your state attorney general.
