# Cookie policy

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This policy explains what Twofold stores on your device and why. It is part of our [privacy policy](#/privacy).

## The short version

- **Twofold doesn't use cookies.**
- It stores a few items in your browser that it needs to work, such as keeping you signed in.
- It uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking** of any kind.
- That's why there's nothing to accept or reject: just a short notice the first time you visit.

## 1. What cookies and local storage are

**Cookies** are small files a website stores in your browser. **Local storage** and **session storage** are similar browser features that let a website keep information on your device. The law treats them the same way: a website may only use them without your consent if they are strictly necessary for something you asked for.

## 2. What Twofold stores on your device

Twofold sets **no cookies**. It uses your browser's storage only for the following:

- **Your sign-in session** (local storage, a key starting with "sb-"), which keeps you signed in. It is kept until you sign out.
- **An offline copy of your planner** ("luma.cache.…"), so the planner opens quickly and works without a connection. It is removed when you sign out.
- **Edits not yet saved** ("luma.pending.…"), so changes made offline aren't lost. They are removed once saved, or when you sign out.
- **Your chosen currency** ("luma.currency"), which remembers the currency you picked on the pricing page. It is kept until you clear your browser data.
- **The plan you picked** ("luma:pendingTier", session storage), so checkout can continue after you sign in. It is removed after checkout, or when you close the tab.
- **That you've read the cookie notice** ("luma.notice.cookies"), so the short note about storage isn't shown on every visit. It is kept until you clear your browser data.

All of these are **strictly necessary** for features you use. None of them track you, and none are shared with anyone. So we don't ask for consent for them.

## 3. Other services

Two services we use may store their own data on your device, under their own policies:

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** shows a spam check on the page where guests answer an invitation. Cloudflare says Turnstile doesn't use cookies for tracking. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Stripe** runs the payment page you go to when you buy a plan. Stripe uses cookies on its own pages to prevent fraud. See [Stripe's cookie policy](https://stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal).

## 4. How to control or remove it

- **Signing out** removes your session, the offline copy of your planner and any unsaved edits from that device.
- You can delete everything Twofold has stored by **clearing your browser's site data** for Twofold. You'll be signed out, and the next time you open the planner it will load your wedding again from our servers.
- If you block browser storage completely, you won't be able to stay signed in.

How to manage site data in the most common browsers:

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/microsoft-edge-browsing-data-and-privacy-bb8174ba-9d73-dcf2-9b4a-c582b4e640dd)
- [Opera](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/)

## 5. Changes

If we ever start using cookies or similar technology that isn't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
