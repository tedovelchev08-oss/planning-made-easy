# Twofold video ads

Social ads (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), vertical 9:16. Recurring couple, warm romantic comedy,
quiet-luxury apartment. Each ad: a relatable hook in the first 2 seconds, the "it's on my phone
too" moment, the couple saying "Two of us... one plan", then the real end card.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `clip2-flow-original.mp4` | Ad 1, clip 2 as generated in Google Flow (720p, 8s) |
| `twofold-clip2-with-endcard.mp4` | Clip 2 upscaled to 1080x1920 + end card (11s) |
| `twofold-endcard.mp4` | End card alone, 3.5s, silent: logo fades in, then "Two of you. One plan." / "Free to start" |
| `endcard.png` | End card still (1080x1920) |
| `endcard-source.html` | Source of the end card (real wordmark + brand fonts); open in a browser at 1080x1920 to re-render |

Not in the repo: **Ad 1, clip 1** (Kling 3 Pro, 10s, 1080p) lives in the ElevenLabs flow
"IXL5X5ThxKmBnBPXEzGh". Download it from there and keep it next to these files.

Rules that apply to every ad (from `PRODUCT.md`):
- No invented testimonials, numbers of users or ratings.
- Never imply paid features are free. "Free to start" is true; "free invitations" is not.
- On-screen text (captions, logo, tagline) goes on in the editor, never generated by the AI.
- The couple are AI actors. This is a deliberate exception to "no stock photos of people", for
  sketch ads only.

## Tips for Flow

- If "Twofold" comes out as "Two fold" with a pause, regenerate. Free credits refresh daily.
- If the faces drift, take a sharper screenshot of each face (front-on, well lit) as the ingredient.
- Generate both clips before editing, and pick the best take of each.
